External risk context — the DRC outbreak
The Bundibugyo virus outbreak in the DRC stands at 6,186 confirmed cases and 3,007 deaths as of 1 September 2026, with 830 patients hospitalised in isolation — the second-largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded. WHO and Africa CDC have declared it a PHEIC and a Public Health Emergency of Continental Security; imported cases have been managed in Germany (May and July 2026) and France (June 2026). Separately, the clade Ib mpox epidemic has recorded 54,116 confirmed cases since January 2024, with Africa CDC warning it continues to spread.

The number AFENET should be reading closely: Africa CDC reports only about 10% of expected contacts listed, ~70% of those followed against a 95% target, ~four in five new infections outside known chains, and >60% of deaths in communities. The risk is narrative absence, and the counter-argument has still never been made.
